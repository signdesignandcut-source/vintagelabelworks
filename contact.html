Ask about a label

Tell me about the machine and attach photos of the old label, even if it's barely readable. I'll reply by email with a price.

Straight-on photos in daylight work best. Photos are shrunk before sending.

Good to include

  • A photo of the whole label, straight on
  • Close-ups of small text or numbers
  • The label's width and height, or a ruler in the photo
  • How many you need

I don't make VIN plates, serial number or certification tags.
