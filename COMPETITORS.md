# Competitors (checked Nov 2026)

## Von Industrial (vonindustrial.com)
- Wix site with three pages: Welcome, Gallery, Contact. **No shop, no prices, no cart.** Every job starts with the contact form.
- Specialty: **deep-etched aluminum plates with powdercoat color fill**, often for high-end one-off work: Brown & Sharpe, Kearney & Trecker, Monarch lathe, Norton, Mori Seiki, a Worthington locomotive plate, a Dee-Wite speedboat hull tag, memorial and custom shop plates.
- Commercially etched batch runs need a **minimum of 6 copies**.
- Strength: every gallery photo has a short story ("borrowed plate to recreate one missing from a steam locomotive restoration").
- Weakness: a buyer can't see prices or buy anything without emailing.

## Vintage Delta Supply (vintagedeltasupply.com)
- Shopify store with 145 products, mostly **parts** (knobs, switches, tool rests, lenses). Labels are a side line: **21 items**.
- **Delta / Rockwell only.**
- Prices:
  - Decals: **$5 each**, or 5 for **$20**
  - Badges: **$15–28**
  - Switch plates: **$10–33**
  - Name plates: **$19.50–32.60**
  - Delta Triple Duty Grinder name plate: **$32**
- Product pages explain the material ("opaque" decal, how to apply it).
- Strength: real cart, clear prices, and it sells grinder parts too, which puts it in front of grinder restorers.
- Weakness: one brand only, no custom work, no before/after photos.

## Where MattBlast fits
Neither competitor does all of: **online shop with prices + custom work with photo upload + hundreds of before/after photos**. The site already has all three.

1. **Grinder specialist, many brands.** Vintage Delta Supply covers one brand. Lead with grinders across all makes, with "shop by brand" and model names in product titles. People search things like "Delta Triple Duty grinder decal".
2. **Show prices.** Von Industrial hides them. Rough market range:
   - Simple decals: $5–8
   - Detailed labels: $15–25
   - Metal plates: $20–35
   - Offer bundle pricing (e.g. a full machine set, 5-packs).
3. **Tell the story of each job** in the gallery, like Von Industrial does. Your Instagram captions can supply this.
4. **Explain materials** on every product, and how to apply them.
5. A metal plate option (etched aluminum) puts you up against Von Industrial on high-end work if you can source it.
